FAQ

Before you buy.

Does it work in FL Studio?

Yes. NOCTIS is a VST3, so it works in FL Studio and any Windows DAW that supports VST3 (Ableton, Reaper, Studio One, Cubase…).

Does it work on Mac?

The macOS version (VST3 and AU for Logic/GarageBand) is in beta. It isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time you open the installer you'll need to right-click it and choose Open.

How do I activate my license?

After buying you get an email with your key. Open NOCTIS, press PASTE and ACTIVATE. Internet is only needed that first time; then it works offline.

On how many computers can I use it?

Each license works on 1 computer. If you switch computers, press “Release this PC” on the old one and activate it on the new one. If your computer broke, contact us.

Can I try it first?

NOCTIS needs a license to work. Watch it in action on the GIANBEAT YouTube channel before buying.

Can I use the MIDI in songs I sell?

Yes, 100% royalty-free. What you create with NOCTIS is yours.
